Walk the neighbourhood, go home to your 4th-floor apartment (street door of 17A, up the stairs), go into the stairwells, ride the Honda CBF600, drive the parked cars or the excavator, play disc golf in Torshovdalen and petanque in the park, take photos, and start the hostage-rescue mission at the red marker beside the entrance of number 17A. Play together: up to five players per lobby – your lobby code is in the pause menu.
